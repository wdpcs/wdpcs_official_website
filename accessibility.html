Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to ensuring an accessible, inclusive, and user-friendly digital environment for all members and visitors.

Review Record: Last Reviewed: [DATE] | Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208

Institutional Commitment

The Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208 is committed to making this official website accessible and usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including individuals with disabilities and those using assistive technologies.

Accessibility Provisions on this Website

  • Accessible Navigation: Intuitive page hierarchies, persistent header menus, structured sitemaps, and breadcrumb trails on internal pages.
  • Keyboard Accessibility: Full support for keyboard navigation using the Tab key, prominent focus rings, skip-to-content links, and Escape-key closures for modals.
  • Readable Typography & Dynamic Font Resizer: Clear font scale and high-contrast letterforms with an interactive font resizer (A- / A / A+) in the top utility bar.
  • Adequate Colour Contrast: Carefully tuned dark navy text and borders on clean neutral backgrounds to prevent colour-only information reliance.
  • Alternative Text for Images: Descriptive alt attributes for institutional emblems, facilities, and photo gallery placeholders.
  • Accessible Forms: Explicit <label> associations, clear error alerts, and accessible captcha verification.
  • Descriptive Links: Clear link destination text rather than ambiguous "click here" labels.
  • Responsive Layout: Flexible multi-device presentation without horizontal scrolling across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
  • Screen-Reader Considerations: Semantic HTML5 landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>, <article>) with ARIA attributes.

Accessibility Feedback

  • Other accessibility or usability barriers.
