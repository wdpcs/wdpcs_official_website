Member Welfare Scheme

About Santhwanam

Santhwanam is a welfare initiative operated by Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208 to extend financial support to its members and their families during difficult times.

The scheme provides financial assistance towards eligible medical treatment expenses and financial support to the eligible family members or claimants in the event of a member's death.

Designed around the values of mutual support, compassion and collective responsibility, Santhwanam reflects the society's commitment to the welfare and financial security of its members.

Benefit Categories

Medical Treatment Assistance & Death Benefit

Overview of welfare support categories, application steps, and required documentation.

A. Welfare Support Category

Medical Treatment Assistance

Santhwanam provides financial assistance to eligible enrolled members who require medical treatment for qualifying illnesses or medical conditions.

Treatment Assistance Information

Financial assistance is extended to eligible enrolled members undergoing treatment for specified medical conditions and qualifying medical emergencies, strictly subject to applicable scheme provisions and Society approval.

Application Procedure

Obtain and complete the prescribed Santhwanam Request for Treatment Aid form and submit it together with treatment bills and required supporting records at the Society office counter during official working hours.

Required Supporting Documents

Completed prescribed request form, member identification and Santhwanam enrolment particulars, hospital/treatment expense records, and any supporting documents requested by the Society. Please contact the Society for current requirements.

Medical Certification Requirements

Relevant medical certificates, diagnostic summaries, discharge summaries, or hospital/physician attestation verifying the medical condition and treatment, where applicable under scheme provisions.

Apply for Treatment Aid →
B. Family Support Category

Death Benefit

In the unfortunate event of the death of an enrolled member, Santhwanam provides financial assistance to eligible family members or claimants in accordance with the applicable scheme provisions.

Death Benefit Information

Financial assistance up to the applicable scheme benefit is provided to support eligible family members or claimants upon the demise of an enrolled member, subject to applicable scheme provisions. Contact the Society for current applicable terms.

Benefit Processing Procedure

No separate application form is required for a Death Benefit claim. Eligible family members, registered nominees, or claimants may contact or visit the Society office directly with the required supporting records.

Supporting Document Requirements

No application form is required. Provide the official death certificate of the enrolled member, Society membership and Santhwanam enrolment details, claimant identification documents, and any additional verification documents requested by the Society.

Eligible Claimant Information

Benefits are considered exclusively for eligible family members or claimants in accordance with official Society records and applicable scheme provisions. Please contact Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208 for official confirmation.
